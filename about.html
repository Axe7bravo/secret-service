THE DIRECTIVE

Operational Ethos

"Discretion is not our policy. It is our currency."

Established as a specialized logistics and experiential agency, Secret Service operates at the intersection of theatre, precision logistics, and absolute secrecy. We craft dispatches that are felt, remembered, and talked about, while leaving the sender entirely in the shadows.

With our primary operational node focused in South Africa, we lead covert dispatches spanning Cape Town to Johannesburg. Our agents are selected for their poise, deadpan execution, and ability to handle high-friction social situations without breaking character.

From delivering custom secret admirer missives to deploying intricate office prank scenarios, every operation is audited and executed with strict compliance to the client's guidelines. We do not keep logs. We do not reveal sources.
